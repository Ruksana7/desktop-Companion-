Place your transparent-background drink animation GIF in this folder:

  drink.gif  - the character walking in and drinking; plays once, then
               the pet goes blank until the next hydration reminder fires.

There is no idle GIF/state — the pet is invisible between reminders by
design.

Notes:
- The GIF should use transparency (not a solid background color) so the
  window shows only the character over the desktop.
- The window automatically resizes to match drink.gif's real aspect
  ratio (capped to AppConfig.maxPetDimension), so it no longer needs to
  match a fixed square box.
- After adding/replacing the GIF you must rebuild (swift build / swift run)
  since SwiftPM copies resources into the build output at build time.

Delete this file once your real asset is in place (harmless to leave it —
it just gets copied alongside the GIF).
